Data Sources

Datasets & Platforms

The geospatial, hydrological, and socio-economic data that powers this pipeline

📍 SOI Well Locations (Yashveer)

Survey of India — provided by Yashveer

Ground-truth well location data from the Survey of India (SOI), used to generate labeled training points. Contains real borewell coordinates within the Anantapur AOI. 278 positive well locations used in this study.

🛰️ Google Earth Engine

earthengine.google.com

Platform for exporting satellite composite data. Used to construct the 6-band GeoTIFF from SRTM, MERIT Hydro, ESA WorldCover, CHIRPS, and Sentinel-2 sources.

🏔️ SRTM 30m DEM

USGS — usgs.gov

Shuttle Radar Topography Mission elevation data at 30-meter resolution. Provides slope and terrain derivatives for Bands 1 and 2.

🌊 MERIT Hydro v1.0.1

Yamazaki et al.

Global hydrography dataset providing upstream area and flow accumulation. Used for TWI calculation (Band 2) and distance-to-stream (Band 3).

🌍 ESA WorldCover v200

European Space Agency

Global land use / land cover classification at 10-meter resolution. Resampled to 30m for Band 4 of the composite.

🌧️ CHIRPS Daily Rainfall

UC Santa Barbara — CHG

Climate Hazards InfraRed Precipitation with Station data. Multi-year (2020–2025) mean annual rainfall for Band 5.

🌿 Sentinel-2 SR Harmonized

Copernicus — ESA

Cloud-masked, median-composite surface reflectance imagery. NDVI (B8−B4 normalized difference) forms Band 6.

🌐 CoRE Stack

core-stack.org

India's geospatial data platform providing hydrological layers, climate indicators, terrain classification, and socio-economic variables used to inform model design and validate feature selection.

💧 CGWB Groundwater Data

Central Ground Water Board — cgwb.gov.in

District-level groundwater stress, over-exploitation data, and individual well monitoring records including depth and groundwater levels.

📊 India WRIS

indiawris.gov.in

Water Resources Information System API — ground water measurement stations, rainfall data, soil moisture, and groundwater quality measurements.
